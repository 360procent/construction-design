This design studio showcases top-quality building designs, offering proven solutions, comprehensive service, and the documentation required for a building permit.

Construction designs

Advancements and innovations in building materials have led to the introduction of not only new architectural solutions but also concepts designed to refine top-quality construction projects through to the building permit stage. The result is the construction of a house or commercial building characterized by functionality, ergonomics, and safety.

Scope of activities:

  • legal support for investment projects – obtaining decisions on development conditions, building permits, technical specifications for utility connections, approvals, and appeals;
  • custom designs;
  • expansion and modernization projects;
  • adaptations of standard, off-the-shelf designs;
  • multi-disciplinary detailed designs;
  • conceptual designs;
  • diverse residential and service building projects;
  • frame and masonry construction
